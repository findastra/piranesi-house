# Piranesi House v3 — status, 2026-10-11

Built with Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`). Public credit is Astra.

## The world now

- **The Cross of the Four Winds:** a 7×7 labyrinth of domed halls linked by naves and arcades (`ArtSource/gen/plan7.py`,
  `layout7.py`, `world7.py`), with far halls beyond the exits, islands, reefs and the roof way.
- **The Colossus is a real statue:** a museum scan of Athena (Three D Scans, no copyright), weathered in Blender with
  sea wear, salt streaks, a green-brown tide stain and lichen. Her textures are baked onto the scan's own UVs, and she
  stands 13.5 m tall. Her right forearm is an ancient break, so the climb up the invisible footholds now ends on her
  left shoulder: *Rest on the Colossus' shoulder*.
- **Statues use their own textures:** scanned statues carry baked marble, normal and occlusion maps through the
  material manifest (`HouseBuilder.cs` statue loop).
- **Water lilies remodelled:** floating pads with the sinus cut to the stem, veined waxy tops and purple-red
  undersides, white and pink Nymphaea flowers with cupped petals and golden stamens, and a closed bud. Three
  variants, each with a LOD (`ArtSource/gen/real/lily.py`).
- **Calm water inside the House:** the ocean's waves stop at the house bounds and in 24 calm zones over the courts and
  drowned halls.
- **Invisible footholds** spiral round the Colossus in place of the old stair.

## Still to do

1. **Vines:** every roof covered and every outer wall mostly ivy (vine-bloomer: ivy mantle shells, IvyMat shader,
   leaf curtains). Texture renderer written (`ArtSource/gen/real/ivymat.py`).
2. **The other statues from real scans:** Penelope becomes the seated giant, Plato the busts and drowned head, the
   ShepherdBoy the child with cymbals, and Nymph or Athena with hand-modelled wings the angel. Hermes plus a bull-head
   scan becomes the Minotaur. The Elephant, Gorilla and Albatross still need animal scans. The procedural sculpts in
   `ArtSource/gen/sculpt/` were rejected as not real and are kept only for the climb-planning helpers.
3. **Windows as plain slots:** no crossbars.
4. **The great tide:** a 20-minute cycle. Floors stay waist-deep, the water rises to 12 m, then falls to a low tide that
   reveals coral and starfish. Fish schools fill the depth. Living spaces sit above 12 m, and the floor level becomes
   ruins.
5. **Roof aqueduct slides:** follow the buildings only, carved into the stone.
6. **The stair top:** remove the landing, and end the spiral on a platform step with the phone booth.
7. **Hibiscus, wine bottle and food:** remodel realistically. The scanned fruit renders too glossy, so lower its
   smoothness.

## Large assets

The regenerated meshes and textures for the 7×7 world (about 750 MB) live in the Unity project on the build PC and
are regenerated by `ArtSource/gen`. This commit adds the code, data, shaders and the new Colossus and water-lily
assets. The full mesh set was not pushed, to stay inside GitHub's push limits.
